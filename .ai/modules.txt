             ('own' always, 'open' while in reach) and send intents move/act/close. M.ContainerMeta is the
             metatable; M:Container(id) looks one up. Test items: virtual module via Env.Server{files=...}.
  equipment: a container kind 'equipment' (slots M.SLOTS) per player; OnInsert/OnRemove wear armor and give/strip
             weapons; weapons:SetAmmoSource reads ammo items first; movement.speed reduce for load.
  medical: trait medical {treatment, opts, time, othersTime, uses, self, others}; organism:CanTreat (checks) +
           organism:Examine (structured) -> medical:Findings/Options -> net report; actions Tick/Complete for CPR.
  bridge: F1-F4 = gm.ShowHelp/ShowTeam/ShowSpare1 (medical examine)/ShowSpare2 (server replacements).
  loot: sv only; RegisterTable{draws, entries{item|table|nothing, weight, count, state}}, RegisterCrate (inventory place
        kind crate, data.loot=id, respawn refill when empty and unwatched); points per game.GetMap() in storage ns
        'loot' (memory without storage); rule 'loot' (params loot, lootShare) registered in Init when rounds runs:
        modes add it only if ctx:Require('loot') ~= nil. M.Random is stub-able in specs (dice helper in loot_spec).
